Help & Support

How this checklist maps to NDPR / NDPA obligations.

Data Map — record every place personal data enters, lives, and moves to. This is the foundation NDPA/NDPR compliance is built on.

Gap Assessment — score your organization against core NDPR requirements. Anything marked "Not Compliant" or "Partial" needs a plan.

Action Plan — track the remediation work closing each gap, with an owner and a due date.

Compliance Report — a snapshot you can print or save as a PDF for your assessment report and stakeholders.

This tool is an MVP checklist to help you organize evidence — it does not constitute legal advice. For formal compliance sign-off, consult NITDA guidance or a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO).
